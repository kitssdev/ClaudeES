Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Who we are

ClaudeES Publisher is a private tool used by the creator of ClaudeES to publish their own educational videos to their own TikTok account. It has a single user: the account owner.

2. Data we access

When the owner authorizes the app with TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token, a refresh token and the owner's open ID, plus basic creator information needed to post (such as available privacy options and maximum video duration). The app uses the video.publish permission only to upload the owner's own videos.

3. How data is used and stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer and are used solely to upload videos to the owner's account. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

4. Data from other users

The app does not collect, read or store any data about other TikTok users.

5. Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions) and delete the locally stored tokens.
